From the Greek Arete: virtue & excellence

Arenthe (uh-REN-thee) stems from the ancient Greek concept of Arete: virtue, moral excellence, and fulfillment of purpose. It stands for stewardship, integrity, and the steady work of building well.

Guardian of Virtue. Not excellence as a slogan. Excellence as the standard we hold ourselves to on every project, partnership, and decision.
