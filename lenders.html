Sponsor Experience

An owner-operator’s perspective

Arenthe Holdings began as Mills & Co. Real Estate in 2017. The platform acquired more than seventy-five residential units inside a two-year period, concentrated in Ohio with additional holdings in Texas.

Historical acquisitions focused on small multifamily and single-family properties purchased below replacement cost, repositioned, and primarily held for long-term performance.

Current direction

Real estate development, capital readiness, and community-serving opportunities across the Midwest.

Minority-owned. MBE enterprise application in process.

Underwriting Readiness

Information organized for a serious review

Materials are provided according to the financing request and stage of the opportunity.

01

Borrower & sponsor

Entity information, sponsor background, portfolio history, debt obligations, liquidity and guarantor information as applicable.

02

Project & collateral

Site information, business plan, market context, plans, schedule, valuation support and third-party reports as available.

03

Sources & repayment

Requested financing, sponsor equity, sources and uses, operating assumptions, projections and the proposed repayment strategy.

04

Execution controls

Development budget, contingency, milestone schedule, draw support, project reporting and clear escalation of material changes.
